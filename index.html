Our Services

Clear & Transparent Trade Arrangements

We facilitate smooth international procurement by offering flexible incoterms. Understanding your logistics preference is paramount to a successful bulk transaction.

FOB Free On Board

You assume control of your cargo once it is loaded onto the shipping vessel at an Indian port. We handle all pre-shipment responsibilities.

  • Local transportation to Port
  • Custom clearance & documentation
  • Export packaging & loading

CIF Cost, Insurance, Freight

Ideal for peace of mind. We manage the entire supply chain, including maritime insurance and freight costs, up to your designated destination port.

  • Comprehensive Marine Insurance
  • Pre-paid Ocean Freight
  • End-to-end documentation

CNF Cost and Freight

  • Organized Ocean Freight
  • Assured transit tracking
  • Cost-effective delivery
